Krebs cycle

The Krebs cycle runs in the mitochondrial matrix. Each turn takes one acetyl-CoA from Glycolysis and the link reaction, and gives off two CO2.

What one turn makes

  • 3 NADH and 1 FADH2, which carry electrons to the electron transport chain
  • 1 GTP, which becomes ATP
  • 2 CO2 as waste

Why it matters

Most of the cell's ATP comes later, from the electrons these carriers bring. The cycle itself makes little ATP directly.

One turn, from your notes:

  1. Acetyl-CoA joins oxaloacetate to make citrate.
  2. Citrate is rearranged and loses two CO2 over the next steps.
  3. Along the way, 3 NAD+ become NADH and 1 FAD becomes FADH2.
  4. One GTP is made, then turned into ATP.
  5. Oxaloacetate is rebuilt, ready for the next acetyl-CoA.
