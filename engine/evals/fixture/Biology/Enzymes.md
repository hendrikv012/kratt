# Enzymes

Enzymes are protein catalysts. They speed up reactions without being used up, by lowering the activation energy.

## Active site

The substrate binds to a small pocket called the active site. In the induced fit model the site changes shape slightly around the substrate, which strains its bonds and helps the reaction along.

## Inhibition

A competitive inhibitor looks like the substrate and blocks the active site; more substrate can overcome it. A noncompetitive inhibitor binds elsewhere and changes the shape of the enzyme, so extra substrate does not help.

## Conditions

Each enzyme has an optimum pH and temperature. Heat above the optimum denatures the protein, and the reaction stops for good.
