# Cell membrane

The cell membrane separates the inside of a cell from its surroundings. It is a bilayer of phospholipids with proteins embedded in it.

## Structure

Each phospholipid has a hydrophilic head and two hydrophobic tails, so the tails face inward and the heads face the water. Cholesterol keeps the membrane fluid at different temperatures.

## Transport

Small nonpolar molecules such as oxygen slip through by diffusion. Water crosses by osmosis, partly through aquaporin channels. Glucose uses carrier proteins, and the sodium-potassium pump spends ATP to move ions against their gradient.

## Signalling

Receptor proteins bind hormones on the outside and pass the message inward, often by triggering a second messenger.
