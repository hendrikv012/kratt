# Photosynthesis

Plants, algae and some bacteria turn light, water and carbon dioxide into sugar and oxygen. It happens in the chloroplast.

## Light reactions

Chlorophyll in the thylakoid membranes absorbs light and splits water, releasing oxygen. The energy is stored as ATP and NADPH.

## Calvin cycle

In the stroma, the enzyme rubisco fixes carbon dioxide onto a five-carbon sugar. ATP and NADPH from the light reactions are spent to build glucose from the fixed carbon.

## Limits

Rate depends on light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature. When light is plentiful but carbon dioxide is scarce, the Calvin cycle becomes the bottleneck.
