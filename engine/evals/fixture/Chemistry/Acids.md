# Acids and bases

An acid donates a proton and a base accepts one (the Brønsted-Lowry definition). The pH scale measures how many hydrogen ions a solution holds.

## pH scale

pH is the negative logarithm of the hydrogen ion concentration. Pure water is 7, acids are below 7 and bases above. One pH unit is a tenfold change in concentration.

## Strong and weak

A strong acid such as hydrochloric acid ionises completely in water. A weak acid such as acetic acid only partly ionises, and its strength is given by the dissociation constant Ka.

## Buffers

A buffer is a weak acid with its conjugate base. It resists changes in pH when a little acid or base is added. Blood uses a bicarbonate buffer.
