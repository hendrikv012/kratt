# Periodic trends

Elements in the periodic table are ordered by atomic number, and their properties repeat in a pattern.

## Atomic radius

Atoms get smaller across a period, because the nuclear charge grows while the electrons stay in the same shell. They get larger down a group, as new shells are added.

## Ionisation energy

The energy needed to remove an outer electron rises across a period and falls down a group. Noble gases have the highest values in their period; alkali metals the lowest.

## Electronegativity

Electronegativity is how strongly an atom pulls shared electrons. Fluorine is the highest. It increases across a period and decreases down a group.
