# Reaction rates

The rate of a reaction is how fast reactants turn into products. Collision theory says particles must collide with enough energy and the right orientation.

## Factors

Raising the temperature, concentration or surface area makes collisions more frequent. A higher temperature also gives more particles energy above the activation energy.

## Catalysts

A catalyst offers a path with lower activation energy and is not consumed. Enzymes are biological catalysts.

## Rate law

The rate law links rate to concentrations: rate = k[A]^m[B]^n. The orders m and n are found by experiment, not from the balanced equation.
