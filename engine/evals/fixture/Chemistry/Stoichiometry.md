# Stoichiometry

Stoichiometry uses balanced equations to calculate how much of each substance is used or made.

## The mole

One mole is 6.022 x 10^23 particles. The molar mass in grams per mole turns a mass into a number of moles.

## Limiting reagent

The reactant that runs out first is the limiting reagent. It decides the most product that can form, called the theoretical yield. Divide each amount of moles by its coefficient; the smallest result is the limiting one.

## Percent yield

Percent yield is the actual yield divided by the theoretical yield, times 100. Side reactions and lost product keep it below 100.
